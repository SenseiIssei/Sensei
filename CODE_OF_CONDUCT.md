# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they're acting in good faith. Argue about code,
not about each other.

## Our pledge

We want contributing to Sensei to be a good experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Give people the benefit of the doubt. Most friction online is misreading tone.
- Critique the work, not the person. "This will break X" beats "this is wrong".
- Accept that maintainers have to say no sometimes, and that a "no" to a
  feature isn't a judgement of you.
- Nobody is owed free labour. A PR that goes unreviewed for a while means
  someone is busy, not that you're being ignored.

## What we won't accept

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks — public or private
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or bad-faith argument

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, PRs, discussions, commit messages) and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer via a GitHub direct message or
Discord (`senseiissei`). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers who
don't follow this code are held to it too.

Consequences escalate with severity: a private word, a public warning, a
temporary ban, or a permanent ban.
